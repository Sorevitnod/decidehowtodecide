Architectural leadership prompting diagnostic

Architectural Instincts

This diagnostic looks at the questions you prioritise when faced with ambiguity, pressure and proposed solutions. It shows whether your leadership prompts deeper problem framing, better architectural judgement and stronger collective thinking.

For each scenario, select the two questions you would be most likely to ask first.

There are no deliberately absurd answers. The diagnostic is interested in what you prioritise first — especially whether you remain in problem, evidence and trade-off space before moving into solution space.

A developmental diagnostic, not a psychometric assessment. Scenario and answer order are shuffled each time.

Prompting patterns

Architectural Curiosity reflects how often you probe assumptions, uncertainty, evidence and hidden system behaviour.

Premature Convergence reflects how often you jump toward target architecture, platform, technology or implementation choices before the problem is fully framed.

Generative Leadership reflects how often your questions help the team think, test and challenge rather than simply defer to the leader's answer.

    Science behind your result

    This section deliberately combines leadership practice, architecture practice, behavioural research and decision practice. The architecture evidence adapts to the patterns in your profile.

    What this profile invites you to consider

    Your prompting style may emphasise some parts of the decision process more than others. The value of this profile is in noticing where you tend to spend attention, where you move quickly, and where additional questions might improve the quality of thinking around a decision.
